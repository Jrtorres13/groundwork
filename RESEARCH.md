# Groundwork: research notes

Short, practical findings that shaped the app. Not medical advice.

## 1. Calisthenics (bodyweight strength)
- **Overload without weights:** change leverage (incline → floor → decline), range, tempo, one-side work, then reps.
- **Double progression:** 3 sets x 5 reps → add 1 rep per set each session → at 3 x 8, move to the next, harder variation and restart at 3 x 5. (r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine.)
- **Holds:** add ~5 s per session until the target, then move up.
- **Effort:** stop ~1–2 reps before failure. Rest 90 s–3 min on strength work.
- **Frequency:** full body, 3x/week, non-consecutive days.
- **Balance the patterns:** push, pull (vertical + row), squat, hinge, core (anti-extension, hollow).

## 2. Mobility
- **Mobility = range you can control.** Flexibility = passive range.
- **CARs** (controlled articular rotations, from FRC): slow, full circles of each joint. Daily, 1–3 reps per joint. Good joint "health check".
- **End-range strength:** loaded stretches (pancake, Jefferson curl, cossack) build strength where you're weakest.
- **Strength training improves range of motion about as much as stretching** when done through full ROM (Afonso et al., 2023 meta-analysis).
- **Dose (ACSM):** 2–3+ days/week (daily best). Hold 10–30 s (30–60 s for older adults), ~60 s total per muscle.
- **Timing:** dynamic mobility before training. Long static holds (60 s+) right before explosive work can briefly cut power, so save them for after.

## 3. Fascia
- Fascia = the body-wide web of connective tissue (tendons, ligaments, muscle wrappers). It transmits force and is full of sensory nerves.
- **Schleip & Müller (2013) training principles:**
  1. **Elastic recoil:** springy, bouncy moves with a small counter-movement first (pogo hops, arm swings).
  2. **Slow, multi-direction, long-chain stretches:** reach through whole-body lines, small gentle "melting" variations instead of one fixed angle.
  3. **Hydration / renewal:** slow foam rolling or ball work.
  4. **Proprioceptive refinement:** slow, mindful movement, explore angles, notice sensation.
- **Dose:** ~10–20 min, 1–2x/week is enough. Collagen adapts slowly: real change takes **6–24 months**, but lasts.
- **Evidence:** good for elastic recoil + proprioceptive training. Combined "fascia programs" are less proven. Foam rolling reduces soreness and gives short-term range gains, without hurting performance.

## 4. Tendons, joints, recovery
- Tendons adapt slower than muscle. Build volume gradually (~10%/week).
- Prep wrists before push-ups and handstands. Hang (dead hang) for shoulder health.
- 48 h between hard sessions for the same muscles. Sleep 7–9 h. Protein ~1.6 g/kg/day (Morton et al., 2018).
- Effort and stretch sensation are fine. Sharp, pinching or joint pain = stop.
